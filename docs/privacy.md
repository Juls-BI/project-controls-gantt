# Privacy policy

**Project Controls Gantt** custom visual for Microsoft Power BI

Effective date: 27 October 2026

## Summary

Project Controls Gantt does not collect, store, share or send any data.

## What the visual does with your data

The visual displays the data that you add to it in a Power BI report. That data is processed only inside Power BI, in your browser or in Power BI Desktop, to draw the chart.

- The visual makes no network requests and does not contact any server, including the author's.
- The visual does not use cookies, local storage or tracking of any kind.
- The visual does not collect personal information, usage statistics or telemetry.
- The visual does not request any special Power BI permissions (privileges).

Data you load into Power BI is handled by Microsoft under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). The visual saves its formatting settings inside your Power BI report, in the same way as Power BI's own visuals.

## Support requests

If you open an issue on the [support page](https://github.com/Juls-BI/project-controls-gantt/issues), the information you write is public and is handled by GitHub under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Do not include confidential or personal information in an issue.
